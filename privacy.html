Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to EarnHub PRO ("we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you visit our survey platform and use our reward services.

2. Information We Collect

To provide high-paying brand surveys and process your rewards securely, we may collect the following data:

  • Personal Identification: Full name and email address provided during registration.
  • Demographic Data: Age, gender, education level, employment status, monthly income bracket, marital status, location, and device preferences filled in your profile hub.
  • Transaction & Reward Data: Wallet balances, referral tracking stats, and gift card redemption history.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is strictly utilized for:

  • Matching you with relevant brand surveys from verified providers like CPX Research and BitLabs.
  • Crediting referral bonuses and processing gift card dispatches to your verified email.
  • Preventing fraudulent activities and duplicate accounts to ensure platform integrity.

4. Data Security

We implement robust security measures via Supabase backend encryption to ensure your personal and demographic data remains safe and protected against unauthorized access.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please reach out to our support team through the platform dashboard.
